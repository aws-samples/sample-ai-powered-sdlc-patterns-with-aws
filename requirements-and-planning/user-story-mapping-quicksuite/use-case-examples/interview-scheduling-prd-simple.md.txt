# Product Requirements Document: Interview Scheduling

## Overview

Add automated interview scheduling to our job management application. Applicants can view recruiter availability and self-schedule interviews instead of coordinating via email.

## Problem

Recruiters and applicants exchange 8-12 emails per interview to find a mutually available time. This takes 3-5 days and creates poor candidate experience.

## Solution

Self-service scheduling where applicants view real-time calendar availability and book interviews instantly.

## Users

**Applicant**: Job seeker who needs to schedule interviews quickly
**Recruiter**: Hiring manager who wants to reduce scheduling overhead

## Key Features

### 1. View Available Time Slots
- Display recruiter's available times for next 30 days
- Show times in applicant's local timezone
- Filter by interview type (phone screen, technical, final)

### 2. Book Interview
- Select available time slot
- Instant confirmation via email
- Calendar invite sent to both parties
- Booking synced to applicant tracking system

### 3. Reschedule/Cancel
- Reschedule up to 24 hours before interview
- Cancel with optional reason
- Automatic notifications sent

### 4. Manage Availability
- Recruiters set weekly availability hours
- Block specific dates for time off
- Set maximum interviews per day
- Sync with Google Calendar or Outlook

### 5. Notifications
- Email confirmation with calendar invite
- Reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before interview
- Notifications for cancellations and reschedules

## Interview Types

- **Phone Screen**: 30 minutes
- **Technical Interview**: 1 hour  
- **Final Interview**: 2 hours

## Requirements

- Mobile responsive (70% mobile users)
- Integrate with existing calendar systems (Google, Outlook)
- Support 500+ applicants per month
- 99.9% uptime during business hours

## Success Metrics

- Reduce time-to-interview from 5 days to 2 days
- Eliminate 90% of scheduling emails
- Achieve 4.5/5 candidate satisfaction score

## Technical Context

- Company: Mid-size (500-1000 employees)
- Recruiters: 15
- Monthly applicants: 500+
- Existing stack: React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, AWS
- ATS: Greenhouse
